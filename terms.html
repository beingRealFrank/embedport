Every job has a fixed price that we confirm in writing by email before work starts. If the work turns out to be bigger than quoted, we tell you before doing any of it, and you can accept the new price or stop and pay nothing for that part.

Payment

Audits are paid on delivery. Store migrations and app ports are paid half up front and half on delivery. If we can't deliver what we quoted, we refund what you paid.

For store migrations we need staff access with theme and app permissions. All changes go on a duplicate theme first. We publish a theme only with your written go-ahead. App ports are delivered as a pull request: nothing merges or deploys unless you do it.
